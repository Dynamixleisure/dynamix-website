How each stage runs

Join at whichever stage your project has reached. Here is what happens in each, and what you hold at the end of it.

1

Start with the numbers

Market sizing, footfall and spend modelling come first. Then the attraction mix, layout and guest flow, and finally positioning and phasing. Nobody draws a facade until the business case holds.

You end up withA concept, a business case and a phased plan that a board and a bank can sign.

2

One team, one opening date

Project delivery, vendor and contractor management, ride safety compliance and audit, systems and staffing. A soft launch stabilises the operation before the public arrives.

You end up withOpening day, with a trained team in place and safety systems already audited.

3

The scoreboard is yours

Daily operations, KPIs and campaigns, retail and food and beverage per-cap spend, planned maintenance and uptime. If a venue is underperforming, a turnaround plan with a deadline.

You end up withA venue that is full at weekends, safe every day and growing year on year.

4

Fix the yield first

The place is built. The brands are signed. The guests are coming. The numbers are not where they should be. We take the operating mandate and work it line by line: pricing and yield, in-venue spend, the content calendar, the cost base, capital structure and governance. No new capex until the yield is fixed.

You end up withA destination that earns more from every guest who was already walking through the door.

Our expertise

Eleven kinds of venue we have designed, built or run. Whatever the attraction, the operating discipline is the same.

Social Entertainment Centres

Bowling, darts, karaoke, mini golf and dining under one roof. Venues built for groups, evenings and repeat visits, where the food and drink spend matters as much as the games.

Indoor / Outdoor Adventure Parks

Ropes courses, climbing walls, zip lines and obstacle runs. High-energy attractions where safety systems, throughput and staff training decide whether the day works.

Hotels & Resorts

Leisure and entertainment offers inside hospitality destinations: kids clubs, activity programmes, water play and attractions that lift length of stay and per-guest spend.

Indoor / Outdoor Water Parks

Slides, wave pools, lazy rivers and splash zones. We plan the ride mix, guest flow and lifeguard operations, and keep the water and the numbers in balance all year.

Family Entertainment Centres

Rides, soft play, arcades and party rooms for families with young children. The bread and butter of the industry, and the venue type our team has run the most.

Indoor / Outdoor Theme Parks

Themed lands, signature rides and shows. From concept and ride procurement to opening day and the tenth season, including licensed IP parks such as Angry Birds World.

Edutainment Centres

Learning disguised as play: role-play cities, science and discovery centres and interactive exhibits. Designed so schools book by the busload and families come back.

Cultural Attractions

Museums, heritage sites and immersive exhibitions. Visitor experience, ticketing, retail and events operated to the standards a cultural institution expects.

eGaming Centres

Esports arenas, LAN centres, sim racing and VR. Home of Virtuocity in Doha, where competitive gaming meets events, tournaments and community.

Fitness & Extreme Sports Centres

Trampoline parks, skate and bike parks, surf and snow. Adrenaline attractions where membership models, safety protocols and coaching turn thrill-seekers into regulars.

Airports

Terminals, concessions, lounges and airside attractions. Turning the hours between check-in and boarding into time passengers enjoy, and spend in.
